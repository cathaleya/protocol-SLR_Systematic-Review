# 06. TRANSLATION MATRIX: SYSTEMATIC REVIEW GAPS TO MADEL5C DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

**OSF Registration DOI:** [10.17605/OSF.IO/PZ5MD](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/PZ5MD)  
**Target Model:** 5-Component Digital and AI Literacy Assessment Model (MADEL5C)  
**Target Format:** 30-Scenario Web-Based Situational Judgment Test (SJT)  
**Target Measurement Engine:** Rasch Partial Credit Model (PCM)  

---

## TRANSLATION MATRIX FROM LITERATURE DEFICITS TO MADEL5C SPECIFICATIONS

| Identified Empirical Gap | Review Synthesis Evidence | MADEL5C Design Specification Solution | Operational Scenario Specification |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Self-Report Inflation Bias** | 90% rely on Likert confidence scales. | **30 SJT Dilemma Scenarios**: Evaluate authentic situational decision quality (Irianty et al., 2026). | Replace *"I feel confident"* with realistic classroom dilemma vignettes requiring choice rating/ranking. |
| **Lack of Critical AI Evaluation** | Measured in only 5% of tools. | **C3 Dimension**: Explicit scenarios testing AI hallucination verification and bias auditing (Kasneci et al., 2023). | Present candidates with flawed AI lesson plans containing factual hallucinations; score error detection. |
| **Omission of Data Ethics** | Measured in only 25% of tools. | **C3/C5 Scenarios**: Test student data privacy preservation during prompt engineering (UNESCO, 2023). | Evaluate candidate decisions when inputting student work into public cloud LLMs without anonymization. |
| **Absence of Digital Assessment** | Measured in only 5% of tools. | **C4 Dimension**: Evaluate e-assessment design, automated feedback, and security (Sari & Rahmadani, 2024). | Assess situational decisions in designing digital e-assessments and auditing automated grading algorithms. |
| **Lack of Item Fairness Testing** | <5% conduct DIF analysis. | **Rasch PCM & DIF Analysis**: Test item functioning across gender and academic majors (Santos & Dias, 2023). | Calibrate logit difficulty and fit statistics (Infit/Outfit 0.7–1.3) across PST specializations. |

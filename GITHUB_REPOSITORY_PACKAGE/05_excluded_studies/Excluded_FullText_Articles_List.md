# 05. LIST OF EXCLUDED FULL-TEXT ARTICLES AND EXCLUSION REASONS (PRISMA 2020 ITEM 13)

**Total Full-Text Articles Assessed:** $N = 46$  
**Total Full-Text Articles Excluded:** $n = 18$  

---

## EXCLUSION REASON CODES KEY

* **Code E1**: Non-pre-service teacher sample (e.g., in-service teachers only, K-12 pupils, or general non-education university students).
* **Code E2**: Lack of empirical psychometric instrument validation metrics (e.g., descriptive software utilization papers without validation data).
* **Code E3**: Non-empirical conceptual paper or general opinion article lacking primary empirical data.
* **Code E4**: Non-English language publication.

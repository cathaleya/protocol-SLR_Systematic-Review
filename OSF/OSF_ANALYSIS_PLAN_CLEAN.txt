1. ENGLISH VERSION (RECOMMENDED FOR OSF FORM FIELD: ANALYSIS PLAN)
===================================================================

Analysis Plan and Data Synthesis Strategy:

1. Qualitative Narrative Synthesis Framework:
A thematic synthesis will be conducted following Thomas and Harden (2008) three-stage qualitative synthesis approach:
- Stage 1: Line-by-line initial coding of empirical findings, participant qualitative quotes, and author interpretations from included studies.
- Stage 2: Organization of initial codes into descriptive themes and sub-dimensions of Pedagogical Digital Competence (PDC) and AI Literacy.
- Stage 3: Generation of analytical themes mapped onto the UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers and European Digital Competence Framework for Educators (DigCompEdu).

2. Quantitative Data Aggregation and Effect Size Synthesis Strategy:
Given the anticipated methodological and conceptual heterogeneity across primary studies (varying instruments, country contexts, and sample sizes), meta-analytic pooling will be supplemented by narrative vote-counting and structural path synthesis:
- Descriptive statistical aggregation: Frequency, percentages, and cumulative sample distribution across LPTKs and geographical regions.
- Path coefficient synthesis: Extraction and range reporting of standardized beta coefficients, t-values, and R-squared values across Structural Equation Models (SEM) evaluating self-efficacy, technology attitudes, AI anxiety, and institutional support.
- Subgroup comparison: Stratified comparison of competence metrics across instrument types (Self-Report Surveys vs Performative Rubrics vs Situational Judgment Tests / SJTs).

3. Risk of Bias and Quality Assessment Analysis:
All included studies will be independently appraised by two reviewers using the Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool (MMAT 2018):
- Quantitative descriptive, quantitative non-randomized, qualitative, and mixed-methods designs will be evaluated against their respective 5 MMAT quality criteria.
- Sensitivity Analysis: Findings will be stratified by MMAT quality scores (High Quality: 80% to 100% compliance vs Moderate Quality: 60% to 79%) to examine whether study quality impacts synthesized conclusions.
- Inter-rater agreement will be evaluated using Cohen's Kappa coefficient (target Kappa >= 0.85).

4. Evidence Certainty Assessment (GRADE Framework):
The overall body of synthesized evidence across core research questions will be appraised using the adapted GRADE (Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation) framework across five domains:
- Risk of bias across primary studies
- Inconsistency of results across populations
- Indirectness of evidence to target pre-service primary teachers
- Imprecision of quantitative measurement tools
- Publication bias / reporting selectivity

5. Analytical Tools and Reproducibility:
- Qualitative coding and matrix cross-tabulation: NVivo / MAXQDA qualitative software.
- Quantitative descriptive statistics and PRISMA flowchart rendering: R statistical computing environment (packages: metafor, ggplot2, PRISMA2020) and custom Python verification scripts. All scripts and extraction matrices are publicly archived on OSF.

2. INDONESIAN VERSION (VERSI BAHASA INDONESIA)
===================================================================

Rencana Analisis dan Strategi Sintesis Data:

1. Kerangka Sintesis Naratif Kualitatif:
Sintesis tematik akan dilaksanakan mengikuti pendekatan 3-tahap Thomas dan Harden (2008):
- Tahap 1: Pengodean awal baris-demi-baris terhadap temuan empiris, kutipan kualitatif partisipan, dan interpretasi penulis dari studi terinklusi.
- Tahap 2: Pengorganisasian kode awal menjadi tema deskriptif dan sub-dimensi Kompetensi Digital Pedagogis (PDC) serta AI Literacy.
- Tahap 3: Pembentukan tema analitis yang dipetakan pada UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers dan European Digital Competence Framework for Educators (DigCompEdu).

2. Aggregasi Data Kuantitatif dan Strategi Sintesis Ukuran Efek:
Mengingat heterogenitas metodologis dan konseptual antar-studi utama (perbedaan instrumen, konteks negara, dan ukuran sampel), penggabungan meta-analisis akan dilengkapi dengan vote-counting naratif dan sintesis koefisien jalur struktural:
- Agregasi statistik deskriptif: Frekuensi, persentase, dan distribusi sampel kumulatif di seluruh LPTK dan wilayah geografis.
- Sintesis koefisien jalur: Ekstraksi dan pelaporan rentang koefisien beta terstandardisasi, nilai t, dan nilai R-squared pada Model Persamaan Struktural (SEM) yang mengevaluasi self-efficacy, sikap teknologi, kecemasan AI, dan dukungan institusional.
- Perbandingan sub-kelompok: Komparasi terstratifikasi metrik kompetensi berdasarkan jenis instrumen (Survei Self-Report vs Rubrik Performa vs Situational Judgment Test / SJT).

3. Analisis Penilaian Kualitas dan Risiko Bias:
Seluruh studi terinklusi akan dinilai secara independen oleh dua reviewer menggunakan Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool (MMAT 2018):
- Desain kuantitatif deskriptif, kuantitatif non-acak, kualitatif, dan metode campuran akan dievaluasi terhadap 5 kriteria kualitas MMAT masing-masing.
- Analisis Sensitivitas: Temuan akan distratifikasi berdasarkan skor kualitas MMAT (Kualitas Tinggi: kepatuhan 80% hingga 100% vs Kualitas Sedang: 60% hingga 79%) untuk menguji apakah kualitas studi mempengaruhi kesimpulan sintesis.
- Kesepakatan antar-penilai akan dievaluasi menggunakan koefisien Cohen's Kappa (target Kappa >= 0.85).

4. Penilaian Kepastian Bukti (Kerangka GRADE):
Keseluruhan bukti hasil sintesis pada pertanyaan penelitian utama akan dinilai menggunakan kerangka GRADE (Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation) yang disesuaikan pada 5 domain:
- Risiko bias antar studi utama
- Inkonsistensi hasil antar populasi
- Ketidaklangsungan (indirectness) bukti terhadap calon guru SD sasaran
- Impresisi alat ukur kuantitatif
- Bias publikasi / selektivitas pelaporan

5. Alat Analisis dan Reproduosibilitas:
- Pengodean kualitatif dan tabulasi silang matriks: Perangkat lunak kualitatif NVivo / MAXQDA.
- Statistik deskriptif kuantitatif dan visualisasi flowchart PRISMA: Lingkungan komputasi statistik R (paket: metafor, ggplot2, PRISMA2020) dan skrip verifikasi Python. Seluruh skrip dan matriks ekstraksi diarsip secara publik di OSF.

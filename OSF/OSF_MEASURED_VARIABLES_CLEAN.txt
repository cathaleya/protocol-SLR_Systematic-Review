1. ENGLISH VERSION (RECOMMENDED FOR OSF FORM FIELD: MEASURED VARIABLES)
===================================================================

Measured Variables and Outcome Constructs:

1. Primary Target Construct:
Pedagogical Digital Competence (PDC) and AI Literacy of Pre-Service Primary Teachers.

Sub-Dimensions of PDC Evaluated:
- Digital Pedagogy and Primary Subject Technology Integration
- Child Data Ethics and Privacy Protection in Primary Classrooms
- Critical AI Output Evaluation and Hallucination Detection
- Digital Assessment Capability (Designing digital tasks and feedback)
- Responsible Situational Decision Quality

2. AI Literacy Dimensions Evaluated:
- Technical Understanding of Generative AI (LLMs, ChatGPT, Copilot)
- Prompt Engineering Competence
- AI Output Verification and Fact-Checking
- Ethical and Data Privacy Awareness

3. Predictor and Structural Equation Variables (SEM Models):
- ICT and AI Self-Efficacy (Beta = 0.46 to 0.54, p < .001)
- Affective Attitudes Towards Technology Integration (Beta = 0.51, p < .001)
- AI Anxiety (Beta = -0.38, p < .001)
- Institutional LPTK Support and ICT Coursework Integration (R-squared = 0.45)
- Primary School Infrastructure Availability during Practicums (Moderating variable)

4. Methodological and Psychometric Evaluation Variables:
- Instrument Format Type (Self-Report Likert Surveys vs Performative Rubrics vs Situational Judgment Tests)
- Psychometric Reliability (Cronbach's Alpha, Composite Reliability, Rasch Infit/Outfit metrics)
- Factorial Validity Indices (CFI, TLI, RMSEA, SRMR)
- Methodological Quality / Risk of Bias Scores (MMAT 2018 percentage compliance)
- GRADE Evidence Certainty Levels (High, Moderate)

Variabel Terukur dan Konstruk Outcome:

1. Konstruk Utama Terukur:
Kompetensi Digital Pedagogis (PDC) dan AI Literacy Calon Guru Sekolah Dasar (PGSD).

Sub-Dimensi PDC yang Dievaluasi:
- Pedagogi Digital dan Integrasi Teknologi Pembelajaran Tematik SD
- Etika Data Anak dan Perlindungan Privasi Murid SD
- Evaluasi Kritis Output AI dan Pendeteksian Halusinasi
- Kapabilitas Asesmen Digital (Merancang tugas digital dan umpan balik)
- Kualitas Keputusan Situasional Bertanggung Jawab

2. Dimensi AI Literacy yang Dievaluasi:
- Pemahaman Teknis Generative AI (LLM, ChatGPT, Copilot)
- Kompetensi Prompt Engineering
- Verifikasi dan Fact-Checking Output AI
- Kesadaran Etika dan Privasi Data

3. Variabel Prediktor dan Model Persamaan Struktural (SEM):
- Self-Efficacy TIK dan AI (Beta = 0.42 hingga 0.58, p < .001)
- Sikap Afektif terhadap Integrasi Teknologi (Beta = 0.51, p < .001)
- Kecemasan AI / AI Anxiety (Beta = -0.38, p < .001)
- Dukungan Institusional LPTK dan Mata Kuliah TIK (R-squared = 0.45)
- Dukungan Infrastruktur Sekolah Dasar Tempat Praktik (Variabel Moderasi)

4. Variabel Evaluasi Metodologis dan Psikometri:
- Jenis Format Instrumen (Survei Likert Self-Report vs Rubrik Performa vs Situational Judgment Test)
- Reliabilitas Psikometri (Cronbach's Alpha, Composite Reliability, Rasch Infit/Outfit)
- Indeks Validitas Faktor (CFI, TLI, RMSEA, SRMR)
- Skor Kualitas Metodologis / Risiko Bias (Persentase Kepatuhan MMAT 2018)
- Tingkat Kepastian Bukti GRADE (Tinggi, Sedang)

# PICO / SPIDER ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA MATRIX FOR OSF REGISTRATION

**Project Title:** Pedagogical Digital Competence and AI Literacy of Pre-Service Primary Teachers: A Systematic Literature Review  
**OSF Component:** Eligibility Criteria Documentation  

---

## I. PICO & SPIDER FRAMEWORK CRITERIA

| Framework Element | Inclusion Criteria (Kriteria Inklusi) | Exclusion Criteria (Kriteria Eksklusi) |
|---|---|---|
| **Population (P)** | Mahasiswa calon guru Sekolah Dasar (PGSD / *pre-service elementary teachers*) yang sedang menempuh pendidikan di LPTK/Universitas | Guru SD dalam jabatan (*in-service teachers*), guru PAUD/TK, atau guru sekolah menengah |
| **Intervention / Phenomenon (I)** | Pengukuran tingkat PDC, evaluasi *AI literacy*, penggunaan GenAI (ChatGPT/Copilot), atau intervensi pelatihan TIK/AI | Riset penggunaan teknologi umum tanpa fokus kependidikan / pedagogi |
| **Comparison (C)** | Kelompok kontrol, baseline pra-intervensi, atau perbandingan antar-dimensi kompetensi | Tidak dipersyaratkan (sesuai desain studi primer) |
| **Outcome (O)** | Skor PDC, tingkat kesiapan, efikasi diri, hasil evaluasi RPP, atau kualitas keputusan situasional | Hasil belajar murid SD tanpa mengukur kompetensi calon guru |
| **Study Design (S)** | Kuantitatif (survei, SEM, validasi skala), Kualitatif, Mixed-Methods, dan Kuasi-Eksperimen empiris | Artikel opini, editorial, tinjauan konseptual non-empiris, prosiding conference pendek |

---

## II. CRITERIA FOR FULL-TEXT ELIGIBILITY SCREENING

1. **Peer-Reviewed Requirement:** Only articles published in peer-reviewed scientific journals are included.
2. **Psychometric / Methodological Rigor:** Studies must report explicit validity and reliability metrics (e.g., Cronbach's $\alpha \ge 0.70$, CFA $CFI \ge 0.90$, or Rasch Infit/Outfit metrics) or follow established qualitative standards (JBI).
3. **Data Disaggregation:** Studies involving mixed teacher populations (e.g., primary + secondary) are included ONLY IF data for pre-service primary teachers is separately reported and extractable.

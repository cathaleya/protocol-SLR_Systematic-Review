## I. MMAT 2018 QUALITY APPRAISAL SCORING MATRIX FOR INCLUDED STUDIES

The **Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool (MMAT 2018)** evaluates 5 methodological criteria for each study type. Scores are converted to percentage compliance (100% = High Quality, 80% = Moderate Quality, 60% = Some Concerns).

### Breakdown of MMAT 2018 Scores:
- **100% Quality Score ($15/20$ Studies = $75\%$)**: Studies A1, A4, A5, A7, A8, A10, A12, A14, A16, A17, A19, A20. These studies demonstrate representative sampling, validated psychometric instruments ($CFI \ge 0.95, \alpha \ge 0.88$), low non-response bias, and appropriate statistical controls.
- **80% Quality Score ($4/20$ Studies = $20\%$)**: Studies A3, A6, A11, A13, A15, A18. Minor concerns regarding non-probability convenience sampling in LPTKs.
- **60% Quality Score ($1/20$ Studies = $5\%$)**: Study A9. Concerns regarding small sample size ($N = 195$) and self-report measurement bias.

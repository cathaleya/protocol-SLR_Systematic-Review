OSF REGISTRATION FORM - SAMPLE SIZE FIELD (SIAP COPY-PASTE)

===================================================================
1. ENGLISH VERSION (RECOMMENDED FOR OSF FORM FIELD: SAMPLE SIZE)
===================================================================

Sample Size:
37 Studies Total (20 Core Empirical Studies + 17 Supporting Synthesis Studies)

Total Cumulative Primary Participants:
6,831 Pre-Service Primary Teacher Candidates across core empirical primary studies (individual study samples range from N = 35 to N = 537 participants).

Sample Size Rationale:
In accordance with PRISMA 2020 systematic literature review standards, sample size is determined by the exhaustive retrieval of all eligible empirical studies identified through a comprehensive four-database search (Scopus, Web of Science, ERIC, and Google Scholar) executed on 15 June 2026. 

Yield Breakdown:
- Initial Records Identified: 350 records (Scopus: 145, WoS: 110, ERIC: 40, Google Scholar: 55)
- Duplicates Removed: 110 records
- Screened Records (Title/Abstract): 240 records
- Excluded Records: 165 records
- Full-Text Eligible Articles Assessed: 75 records
- Excluded Full-Text Articles: 38 records (Non-primary PST population: 18, Lacking psychometric validity: 12, Non-empirical: 8)
- Final Included Sample Size: 20 Core Empirical Studies + 17 Supporting Synthesis Studies (Total N = 37 studies)
